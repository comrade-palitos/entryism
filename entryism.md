# Leninist Participation in Bourgeois Elections

The debate of whether or not to vote for the Democratic Party, the "lesser evil", has occupied a significant portion of leftist discourse not just in recent years, but throughout the entire history of Marxist thought. Generally the debate does not center around whether or not incremental reforms such as wage increases, healthcare reforms, etc are sufficient in themselves to constitute socialism, but rather whether they are conducive to the propagation of socialism itself. For example, if these reforms, or the election of a "lesser" evil as opposed to a far-right opponent, are able to successfully move the Overton window, than should we support these moderate candidates instead of a socialist with few other voters or abstaining entirely from elections.
